DejaVu Sans (DejaVuSans.ttf, DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf)

Bundled here so PDF report generation has full Cyrillic, Polish/Czech
diacritics, and general Unicode coverage without depending on whatever
fonts happen to be installed on the host system.

DejaVu fonts are a free, open collection (based on Bitstream Vera) with a
permissive license that allows redistribution and embedding, including in
generated documents. Full license text: https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html
